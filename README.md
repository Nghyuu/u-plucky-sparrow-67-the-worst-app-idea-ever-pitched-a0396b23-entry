## The pitch

> SnailMail lets busy executives hire a trained snail to hand-deliver urgent emails, because nothing says 'this deadline matters' like a mollusk arriving three weeks late with your quarterly report strapped to its shell.

## Description

SnailMail is a premium courier app for busy executives who want their urgent emails delivered by a trained snail. Nothing communicates that a deadline truly matters like a mollusk turning up three weeks late with your quarterly report strapped to its shell. It solves a problem nobody has, with total founder confidence.
